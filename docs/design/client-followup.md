# Catalogue, playback and portable preferences contracts

This reference groups the current presentation contracts. Queue occurrence/transport rules live in [queue composition](queue-composition.md), native ownership in [native integration](native-integration.md), privacy/lifetimes in [hardening](../coding/hardening.md), and host evidence in [UI acceptance](../coding/ui-acceptance.md).

## Public browsing and retained selections

Explicit synchronization retains public tracks and exact label associations/prose under one shared catalogue byte budget and scan epoch. Cached browsing is independent of authentication; private queries/annotations and server audio require the captured source session. Legacy artist/album-only caches explain the explicit synchronization needed to add tracks rather than repeatedly reopening the same incomplete cache. A managed local source may explicitly reconnect its existing engine; no implicit scan occurs on opening Tracks.

Advanced Search retains its submitted expression, draft and confirmed 50-row window in the application session. Route remount reuses the result; explicit Search/Reset invalidates it. Publication captures submitted object, page, account generations and catalogue epochs. Private drafts/results never enter public caches or preference backups. See [search lifetimes](../coding/hardening.md#library-and-search).

CatalogSearch centralizes the advanced-search link and wording. Default Your music views use contextual search; catalogue/detail views retain general search. Tags match personal tag names, moods match localized recipes/tags/genres, and queue search matches visible title/artist/album names. Queue filtering preserves full order/duration and occurrence identity; reorder is unavailable while hidden rows would make movement ambiguous.

## Catalogue controls and artwork

CatalogActions owns reverse/list/optional Cover Flow and the final collection action. Shared catalogue/personal control gaps and dense-row action gaps come from style tokens rather than view-specific geometry. The album view's recent control is a rolling-30-day filter, not a separate Nouveautés menu; see [recent albums](recent-albums-filter.md).

Albums, Compilations and Genres share CoverSize, initially 150 px, bounded to 100–280 px in ten-pixel steps. General preference changes clear view overrides. Overrides/defaults use account preferences and portable backup; guest/device choices remain separate. The leading reset glyph is clickable-colored, double-click restores the general default, keyboard activation also resets, and a tabular value sits to the right. Both sizing controls are disabled in Cover Flow. The animated renderer, residency and transition policy remain independent.

Artists offer editorial list rows and full-height cards. Rows prefer panoramic background artwork with portrait/banner fallback; cards prefer portrait artwork with available-height composition, monochrome logo and two albums selected by personal ratings/favourites, then available covers. Album artwork remains independently navigable. Empty card space opens the artist without intercepting album/Play actions. List precedes Cards in the selector, shared Play triangles remain spinner-free, and names/borders use the same hover accent. See [artist presentations](../manual/en/artists.md).

Composers sits between Genres (piano) and Labels (music note for Composers). Its albums reuse grid/list/Cover Flow and filters without an exhaustive composer-chip list. Sorting and lower clickable Cover Flow letters use the primary credited composer, then title; a one-line Composer caption distinguishes the view. Only graph edition/track/recording/work composer credits qualify: performers/primary artists are not inferred composers. Older absent projections require engine update/restart and explicit synchronization; scanning an old running engine cannot add a capability. Dynamic composer collections retain source-qualified exact identities and refuse cross-source widening.

AlbumCount centralizes locale-reactive singular/plural, with one singular and zero/larger counts plural. Artist/discography/label/sidebar summaries share it. Missing-discography suggestions stay reversible, visually distinct and confined to artist discographies; preference controls their visibility.

## Headers, results and layout

StickyHeader owns fixed control placement, separator, summaries and following content gap. ResultCount and leading summaries share the same token. Filter switches keep identical padding/coordinates in open/closed modes; explanations follow the leading icons and hidden-rail counts centre independently. The common Pagination keeps both accessible arrows visible with boundary disabling and bottom breathing room. Tracks puts page size and Refresh at the leading header edge; its list scrolls independently and paging stays below it.

When no applicable source account exists, account-bound workspaces hide their actual header/addition controls without reserving an empty box. This covers moods, favourites, rated albums, tags, collections, standalone queue/M3U, podcasts, concerts, trophies, diagnostics, analyses and history. Local sessions count; mixed-source Tags/queue accept either. The access notice stays inside the view with shared insets, then a correctly centred empty/retained result. The independent Playback drawer remains available and entries are not erased by header visibility. Connected non-administrator analysis readers retain the separate administrative explanation.

EmptyState uses the view's menu glyph and common icon/text geometry; connected empty History retains header dimensions but hides separator paint. Diagnostic/Trophies content is intrinsically tall enough to expose page-end padding. Long saved articles also size their page/panel to content, retaining the actual bottom inset rather than an ineffective margin on an overflowing bounded child.

CountryGlobe's optional readonly geometry loader supports the real bundled asset and isolated explorer fixtures. Picking/drag/zoom share sphere-radius admission; canvas margins are inert, text is unselectable and focus appears on the sphere, not a rectangular outline. Help uses a centred chevron/gap. Country selection retains modifier union/toggle and keyboard access. Radio's whole country/favourites column scrolls together, with a gap before its scrollbar; narrow layouts use page scrolling.

InlineActionForm/ActionButton share field/action heights and activity geometry for RSS/Wikipedia/forms. ChoiceChips adds its check marker only when selected, growing the pill rather than reserving empty left padding. RefreshButton/DownloadButton share a fixed ActivityGlyph with hover/focus Stop; cancellation retires presentation, not an already dispatched IPC operation. A pending retired IPC still prevents overlapping retries. Statistics and player discovery use one central loader in the relevant content area; M3U content loading preserves its separately scrolling file list.

## Queue, lyrics and transport presentation

The drawer survives Stop and can reopen whenever queue entries remain. Its entries retain occurrence objects across native order/Jump updates, preventing scrolled rows from remounting. Queue number clicks do not start playback; lyric controls stop propagation before their SVG/activity mutation. The white retained lyric glyph is noninteractive; missing words have a muted acquisition action below the album-relative number. Bulk and per-track acquisition have separate activity, cancellation and admission. Jobs belong to the application/account, not drawer visibility; closing it does not cancel them.

Optional lyric reads use guarded non-suspending snapshots so initial loads/replacement/download completion cannot collapse the queue or Playback scene. The ordinary lyric switch retains its paragraph/position while hidden and fits only while visible. Untimed lyrics retain manual selection through clock polls, resetting only on track identity and clamping on replacement documents; no fake synchronization is inferred. Floating lyrics are a separate scene, with foreground-only text, fitted width, zero-to-plus/minus-40-degree orientations and retained entry/drift. Frequency styles and scene selection keep their existing independent controls. See [visualizer performance](../coding/visualizer-performance.md).

PlayerTransport owns common control/waveform rows and a fixed shared footer-height token; radio errors/status cannot move metadata, output or volume controls. A dash duration/idle waveform is inert. Profile-owned volume defaults to 0.5 and persists through the existing preference/backup contract; music/radio/podcast share it without writing preferences on initialization. Pending native changes coalesce unsent values and cannot save into a replacement profile.

Simultaneous publication of selection metadata and confirmed native state prevents new titles appearing with old occurrence indices. Stop retains edited order for explicit replay; account/source reset clears it. Frame consumption, seek tickets, DSP/preload cancellation and output transitions are specified in [audio continuity](audio-continuity.md), not inferred from visual states. Completed legacy Server jobs with no source qualifier retain the canonical Server interpretation for lyric refresh.

## Derivatives, downloads and transient integrity

M3U create/download actions stay visible beside Pistes. Captured metadata-only canonical-file lookup disables redundant creation and enables existing download; lookup failure/unsupported capability remains unknown, not absent. Album totals use complete loaded track evidence, exclude stale previous-album data and mark unknown duration. Collection duration is blank when unknown, `M:SS` below an hour and `H:MM:SS` thereafter, without wrapping hours.

Album integrity runs a fresh exact-edition full decode, preferring flac on the engine host with Aède's decoder fallback. Results appear progressively, stay page-local and do not become stored CRC/analysis evidence. Every state occupies the same title-aligned slot, before playback bars: green OK, red clickable KO diagnostic or neutral dash without reference hash. Clear/recheck/reference change retires disclosure; no download is needed. Saved analysis JSON download sits inside the reusable report before its disclosure control. See [analysis report](analysis-report.md).

DownloadButton confirms publication with a three-second green check, never on task submission/cancellation/failure/retired owner. System Downloads receives M3U, analysis JSON, spectra, podcasts, articles/biographies and saved personal notes with collision suffixes. Notes use bounded `Note-<entity>.md` and server-confirmed Markdown, never unsaved drafts. CSV, themes and preference backups retain native save dialogs. Errors share dismissible Feedback; radio stream/directory messages have an explicit five-second lifetime and wrap above the fixed footer.

Settings > Aède submits one captured-owner whole-library generation plan, serializing the four derivative actions and ordered bulk execution; accepted tasks survive view closure. A lost POST response triggers registry recovery, never blind replay. Capability/role checks control admission, only the three latest terminal results display, and force/recalculate is unavailable while admission/work is active. Public library-folder M3Us remain distinct from private saved exports.

## Private collections, imports and evidence

Collection creation/rename/delete/play reads current owner-private expressions and exact bounded membership; track selections never widen to whole albums. Expansion loads only its album region. Confirmation/draft/download state retires on account replacement. Album-listening evidence is private and shown only in Albums, with a 16 MiB presentation budget and no marker for unknown/disconnected sources; complete evidence removes the pointer-inert corner marker. It does not certify physical audibility or reconstruct discarded legacy listens.

Roon album-export import has explicit private-field mapping and preview; unknown Audirvana/Tune personal formats remain disabled. Portable preferences V2 includes the chosen owner's podcasts/listened evidence and saved articles. Import preview declares provenance/destination/counts, adds absent identities, keeps existing records and preflights both store budgets. V1 anonymous files stay readable; unassigned legacy device records are never silently assigned. Parent-controlled rules/usage remain Aède-owned and excluded from child-editable preferences. See [imports](player-imports.md), [local engine](local-engine.md) and [isolation](../coding/account-isolation.md).

## Component and host verification

Storybook uses real props-only surfaces, fictional media and an explicit module inventory; it imports no account/provider/native capability. Its asset middleware admits only GET/HEAD for the exact public country boundary, not traversal or the whole public directory. These checks establish component behavior, not native playback/device acceptance. [Frontend verification](../coding/frontend-documentation.md) defines procedures and [current state](../coding/current-state.md) records qualified checkpoints.
