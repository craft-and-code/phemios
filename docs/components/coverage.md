## Parental controls

[ParentalControls.stories.tsx](stories/ParentalControls.stories.tsx) covers the controlled `ParentalPolicyForm`, `ParentalSchedule`/`ParentalLimit` and `ParentalArtistPicker`: group search and source qualification, explicit save, closed days, midnight, unlimited versus zero limits, radio/RSS exclusions, global podcast permission, disabled controls during a save and another administrator’s read-only lock without a loading spinner. The shared `LibraryPickerRow` also remains exercised through the existing QueueAdd/QueueSearch stories. The administrative Settings wrapper and server authority are covered separately by scoped controller and backend tests; these browser scenarios use fictional metadata without account or network IO.

[UntimedLyricsControls](../../src/components/UntimedLyricsControls.tsx) is covered by the manual-navigation scenario in [PlaybackLyrics](stories/PlaybackLyrics.stories.tsx), including forward/back navigation across a clock update. [RowActions](stories/RowActions.stories.tsx) exercises the actual action primitives under the media-list contexts and checks shared button/glyph dimensions and gaps. [LyricsIcon](../../src/components/LyricsIcon.tsx) is exercised through the timed [QueueTrackLeading](stories/QueueTrackLeading.stories.tsx) surface.

[TrackDurationTotal](../../src/components/TrackDurationTotal.tsx) has complete, partial and unknown scenarios in [TrackDurationTotal](stories/TrackDurationTotal.stories.tsx). [RadioFavourites](stories/RadioFavourites.stories.tsx) combines the actual globe and saved-station surface to check one scrolling column. QueueTrackRow includes the detached-SVG download/transport regression. FilterBar checks context placement and independent count centring; CountryGlobe checks help-chevron alignment. Feedback checks the radio-specific five-second lifetime, and RadioPlayer retains transport geometry with its dismissible stream notification.
