# Make it yours

Annotations belong to the connected account. The catalogue can be shared without sharing your favourites, notes, ratings, collections or history with other users.

## Annotate without changing files

The heart and stars save immediately. Click the same rating again to clear it. The pencil opens tag and personal-note editing; confirm those changes with Save.

Enter creates a tag. Type several comma-separated tags and press Enter to create them together; a comma alone does not submit. A pill’s cross removes it. Existing tags provide autocomplete; Settings → Tags manages this vocabulary. Notes have visual formatting and a download action when text exists.

## Create a collection that evolves

In views offering the action, refine criteria, click Create collection and choose a name. The collection retains criteria, allowing matching future albums to join.

Collections lets you inspect albums, rename, play or delete a collection after confirmation. Deleting it never deletes music. Duration appears only when known.

## Build a queue

Playlists shows the same queue as the Playback drawer. It starts empty when the app launches. Its plus searches tracks, albums, artists, M3Us or collections. A plus attached to Play appends; Play starts a selection.

Drag handles to reorder occurrences. Numeric, alphabetical and reverse sorting let you try a different order. The red cross removes one occurrence; clearing the queue stops playback. Browse M3U files opens lists in admitted folders and exports accessible to your account. Their paths must resolve through their source.

Changing accounts clears the selected mood and its editing draft. Previous personal pages are hidden while the new account loads. A failed mood read displays its error within that view; it does not require leaving the application.
