# Radio, podcasts and CDs

## Find a radio station

Radio queries Radio Browser independently of an Aède connection. Choose a country with the globe and explore its stations. The globe’s circular arrow recentres the suggested home country; results depend on station-published information.

Click a station to listen. Dragging the dial pauses the station for the tuning sound; releasing starts the selected station. Favourites sit below the globe and belong to your account. Search and listening remain available without an account, without personal favourites. A listed stream can still be unavailable or incompatible.

## Subscribe to a podcast

Add its RSS feed address, then Subscribe. A website or sharing link is not always an RSS feed. Choose an episode to play; listened episodes are distinguished. Download can retain an episode on the machine.

Subscriptions and listened state are private per account on this device and included in the selected account’s Phémios backup. Unsubscribing asks for confirmation.

Pause also applies while the episode is preparing, before sound starts. Resume explicitly restarts preparation if the player had not yet created its audio source.

## Play a CD or image

Audio CD works without an Aède server and without recording its use per account. Connect a drive, insert an audio CD and select it. Controls remain unavailable without recognized hardware. Eject is disabled while playing that disc.

Open image accepts a CUE with BIN, WAV or FLAC. APE additionally requires installed FFmpeg/FFprobe. This does not rip or modify a disc. Supervised accounts retain listening restrictions.

The CD header places Eject between Open CUE image and Refresh. It stays disabled without a detected drive/image and while the selected disc is playing. The main disc and current-track Play controls become Pause and resume at the retained position; another row explicitly starts that track.

The last track has no trailing divider. The timeline waveform builds progressively from audio already read during playback; pause preserves it and unread regions after a seek stay blank. No preliminary full-disc scan is needed.

On macOS, Linux and Windows, CD-Text titles and performers appear automatically when present and readable (ASCII/Latin-1). Missing/unsupported text keeps Track N without blocking audio playback. This is local disc metadata, not an online search; no account or extra software is required for this query. CUE titles work as before. Linux/Windows hardware acceptance and real-disc CD-Text verification remain pending; reader support varies. A connected empty drive can be ejected and Refresh can retry discovery without a disc.

On macOS, automatic Music launch is configured under **System Settings → CDs & DVDs**, visible while an optical drive is connected. Choose the action that opens no application for music CDs (Ignore). Phémios does not change this OS setting. See [Apple’s instructions](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/mchlp1354/mac).

Optical dropouts may involve drive latency, competing applications, the audio output or host load. Successful reinsertion alone does not identify a cause. Retest with other players closed and no compilation running; record the disc, track, time, drive and output if it recurs.

## Find a concert

Concerts requests available dates for artists through JamBase. Enter your key in Settings → External sources. The local cap limits free requests; an empty result does not prove that an artist never performs. Provider terms and limits apply.
